# Raiding Damage

Tracks the damage you deal in every room of the Theatre of Blood, Tombs of Amascut, Chambers of Xeric
and The Fractured Archive, and how it compares to the rest of your team.

- **Side panel**: the current raid updates live, room by room. Every finished raid is saved to a history
  you can filter by raid and switch between all damage and boss-only damage. Each raid shows whether it was
  completed, wiped or left, and who you raided with. Right-click a raid to copy a text summary or delete it.
- **Infoboxes** (optional): your damage in the current room, your share of the room, and your raid total.
- **Chat summaries** (optional): your damage and share when each room ends, and for the whole raid when you leave.
- **Top damage** is marked with a star. It's only given when it's certain, so no single untracked
  player could have done more.

## Seeing everyone's damage

The game only says which hits are *yours*. Everyone else's hits are combined, so with two or more
teammates you see them as one group, e.g. "2 others (avg 34%)". With a single teammate, everything that
isn't yours is theirs, so they're shown by name.

To see each teammate separately, join a [RuneLite party](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/wiki/Party)
together. Party members using this plugin share their damage automatically, which you can turn off with **Share with party**.
